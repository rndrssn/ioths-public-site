## Editorial choices

Explain a specific problem before describing the app's response. Prefer sentences about files, checkboxes, questions, and decisions over phrases such as “thinking practice” or “a smaller surface.” Let paragraphs develop the point rather than repeating it as several short slogans.

The note example follows a changed deadline and an unchanged scope. All three stages retain the original question, so the example demonstrates keeping a task with its context. It is labelled as an example, not presented as the author's personal experience.

First person is limited to the reason for building the app, already stated in the production homepage's “Why this exists” section. No new autobiographical claims, user research, testimonials, or efficacy claims were added.

Game of Life remains an analogy, with the model explained before the connection to ioths. App behavior, storage setup, and the purchase distinction remain factual. The journal's layout and interactions remain in place.

The objective is fidelity to the source voice and useful writing. No AI-detector service was used, no score is claimed, and the copy was not altered with artificial errors to influence a detector.
