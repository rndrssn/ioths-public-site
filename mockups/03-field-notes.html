Your library is where ideas accumulate. Your phone can be where the next one begins.

I / Away from the desk

Three familiar moments.

Small interruptions in the path from noticing something to keeping it.

01

A thought, but no connection.

The train has left the station. A sentence from the book you are reading connects with something you have been working on.

Write it down in ioths. Reading and writing work offline; connecting storage can come later.

02

An observation, but no plan.

You know something is worth keeping. You do not yet know whether it is a question, a task, or the beginning of a longer piece.

Let it be a note. Text, a photograph, or a shared link can hold the context while the idea is still forming.

03

A next step, in the middle of a note.

While writing, you notice something you could do. Moving it somewhere else would separate the action from the reason for it.

Add a checkbox where the thought already lives. Find it again in Tasks, alongside open items from other notes.

II / A small, repeatable practice

Notice first.
Let structure follow.

  1. 01 / Collect

    Keep the observation.

    Write the sentence. Save the context. A note does not need a destination beyond being worth keeping.

  2. 02 / Return

    Find the next step.

    Use tags, pins, and search to return to a thought. Add a checklist when the work becomes clear.

  3. 03 / Attend

    Choose what is active.

    Mark a note in progress. Set a limit on active notes and see the shape of the work on the board.

III / Back at your library

The thought travels
as a file.

ioths stores Markdown with YAML frontmatter: familiar material for an Obsidian library, and readable in a text editor.

Choose a supported storage destination when you want to connect your workflow. The text keeps its usefulness beyond the app that captured it.

Storage requires setup. File compatibility does not automatically connect an existing vault.

A note on the tool

Made for the moment
before it makes sense.

ioths began as a personal need for a focused place to write. Its role is small: help an observation survive the journey back to your work.

For iPhone and iPad. No ioths account. Notes stay on your device unless you activate an optional storage destination.

Local note-taking is free. Optional Files-folder, GitHub, and GitLab storage are part of Full Unlock, a one-time purchase.
