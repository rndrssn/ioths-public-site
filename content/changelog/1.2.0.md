### New

- Kanban can now switch between note cards and tasks grouped under their source note.

- Reaching your work-in-progress limit now lets you adjust the limit instead of automatically moving another note back to Open.

- Long-pressing a pinned note in List view now highlights only that note.
- Files-folder storage now clearly explains that a Files provider must support persistent folder selection.
