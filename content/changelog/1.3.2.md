### New

- Task reminders: give a checklist task a due date and ioths reminds you that morning. Turn notifications on in Your Controls → Reminders, where you can also pick the time of day.
- Set a task's due date by typing @ on the task line, or with the new calendar button above the keyboard. The date reads as quiet text in the editor and turns to the attention colour once the task is overdue.
- Due dates show as a chip on notes, tasks and the Tasks widget. Filter the notes list by Due, sort it by Due date, and group the Tasks screen by Due date.
- Find text inside a note from the note menu.

### Improved

- Clearer translations across all eight languages, with consistent technical terms and more precise storage and recovery instructions.
- A calmer first launch: one screen shows how a thought becomes a note with a next step, then you start writing. Storage choices are no longer part of first launch; they stay in Your Controls whenever you want them. The Feature tour row in Help is gone with it.
- On the Kanban board, the new-note button now starts a task, so the new note appears in Open instead of seeming to vanish. If a note loses its last task, a short notice explains it has left the board, with a shortcut to the List view.
- Kanban cards always use the comfortable layout, so their date and tags stay visible. The Compact view setting now applies to the List and Tile layouts only.
- Compact tiles no longer show tags, leaving a single tidy line with the date.
- Undo and redo now restore Markdown formatting and list edits in the note editor.
- The unlock screen now states that writing and editing on your device stay free.
