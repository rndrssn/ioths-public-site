Offline notes in Markdown

A note doesn’t
need a plan.

You can know that something is worth writing down without knowing what to do with it. ioths is a notes app for iPhone and iPad. It works offline and saves Markdown files, so the note can become part of an existing Obsidian library.

02 / Emergence

What the icon refers to.

What follows is not always visible at the start.

A thought becomes a note before it becomes anything else. Later, a return to that note may make a question, a decision, or a piece of work distinct. The path matters, but it cannot be inferred from the first fragment alone.

Game of Life makes the same constraint visible in miniature. Its rules are fixed and local, yet a pattern’s larger behaviour only becomes legible as it unfolds. In complex systems, the causes remain real; the account of how they combined is often made retrospectively.

The comparison is limited. A note does not cause its own next step. New circumstances and attention do. Keeping the earlier thought makes that connection easier to see and explain.

03 / About

Why I made it.

I built ioths for myself because the notes apps I had tried never quite fitted, either in what they did or how they felt to use.

The result is fairly specific: somewhere to write on the phone, keep the file, and add tasks without copying the thought into another tool. If you already use Obsidian, the file format will be familiar.
